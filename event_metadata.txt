IEEE COSMIC-2025 (2025 IEEE International Conference on Computing, Semiconductor,
Mechatronics, Intelligent Systems, and Communications) was held on November 21-22,
2025 at Sahyadri College of Engineering and Management (SCEM), Mangaluru, Karnataka,
India.

Chief Patrons: Dr. Manjunath Bhandary (Chairman, Bhandary Foundation), Dr. S.
Vidyashankar (Vice Chancellor, VTU Belagavi).
General Chair: Dr. S. S. Injaganeri, Principal, SCEM.
Organizing Chairs: Dr. Mustafa Basthikodi, Dr. Pushpalatha K.
Program Chairs: Sudheer Shetty, Vasudeva Acharya, M. Srinath, Ananth Prabhu G.

Keynote Speakers: Dr. S. Vidyashankar (VTU), Dr. Prasant Misra (Senior Scientist,
TCS Research), Dr. Chengappa Munjandira (Senior Technologist, Hewlett Packard).

Tracks: Computing, Semiconductor, Mechatronics, Intelligent Systems, Communications.
